# Sandwich Construction

## Assumptions:
- There is an unobstructed table infront of you.
- There is several items ontop of the counter:
    - 2 slices of bread on the right, on a plate, placed beside eachother.
    - An opened jar of peanut butter in the middle with a lid infront of it
    - a butterknife laying to the right of the peanut butter
    - a paper towel to the right of the peanut butter.
    - and an opened jar of jelly on the left with a lid infront of it.
- The robot is fully charged.
- A trashcan in the room
- A sink in the room.

## Instructions:
1. Grab the butter knife and lower it verticlly into the jar of peanut butter.
2. Move the knife horizontally, then upwards in a scooping manner.
3. Raise the knife out of the jar of peanut butter.
4. Bring the knife over one of the two slices of bread, lowering it horizontally.
5. Press the peanut butter against the bread and swipe the knife across the bread to the right.
6. Raise the knife vertically and grab the paper towel.
7. Hold the paper towel and pinch the knife with it just above the handle.
8. Move the paper towel forward across the knife until it slides off.
9. Drop the paper towel on the counter.
10. Move the knife to the left over the Jelly.
11. Lower the knife vertically into the Jar of Jelly.
12. Move the knife horizontally, then upwards in a scooping manner.
13. Raise the knife out of the Jar of Jelly.
14. Bring the knife over the uncovered slice of bread, lowering it horizontally.
15. Press the knife against the bread and swipe the knife across the bread to the left.
16. Place the knife down on top of the paper towel.
17. Grab the bread with peanut butter on it, and place it on the Jelly covered bread, peanut butter side face down.
18. Let go.
19. Grab the lid infront of the peanut butter and screw it onto the top of the peanut butter jar.
20. grab the lid infront of the jelly and screw it onto the top of the jelly jar.
21. Put both Jars away, the knife in the sink, and the paper towel in the trash.
21. End.

## Exit Conditions:
- Plate and constructed Sandwich are only items on the counter.
